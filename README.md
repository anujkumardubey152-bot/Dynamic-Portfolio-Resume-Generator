## 📖 About the Project

**Dynamic Portfolio & Resume Generator** is a free web app made with **Python** and **Streamlit**.

You fill in your details one time: name, education, skills, projects, and experience. The app then gives you:

- A clean **PDF resume** that company software can read easily
- A ready-made **portfolio website** that you can download and share
- A **job match score** that tells you how well you fit a job
- A **tracker** to keep a record of all your job applications
- A **dashboard** to see your whole job search in one place

You do not need any coding knowledge. Open the link, fill in the form, and download your files.

## ✨ Features

### 1. Profile Form
Enter your personal details, education, skills, experience, and projects in one simple form. You fill it once and use it for everything.

### 2. ATS-Friendly PDF Resume
Your resume is created in a clean, single-column layout. It avoids tables, heavy graphics, and confusing designs, so hiring software can read every section correctly.

### 3. HTML Portfolio
Get a portfolio web page made from your profile. Download it and put it online for free on GitHub Pages or Netlify. Add the link to your resume and LinkedIn.

### 4. Job Matcher
Paste any job description and the app shows:
- Your match score
- Keywords you already have
- Keywords you are missing

Use this to improve your resume before you apply.

### 5. Application Tracker
Save every job you apply for. You can add, edit, and delete applications, and update the status as things move forward, for example Applied, Interview, Offer, or Rejected.

### 6. Dashboard
A simple summary of all your applications, so you can see how many you applied for, how many are in progress, and what needs your attention.

### 7. Input Validation
The app checks your email, phone number, and links before creating files. This saves you from silly mistakes that can cost you an interview call.

### 8. Privacy Settings
Choose which details appear in your resume and portfolio. For example, show your phone number on your resume but hide it on your public portfolio.

### 9. Admin Access
Some management pages are available only to the app owner after login. Normal users can use all the tools above freely.

## 🧭 Understand the Hiring Process

Many students and freshers do not know what happens after they click "Apply". Here is the full process in simple words.

### Step 1: Job Posting
A company decides it needs a new person. HR writes a **job description (JD)**. It lists the role, the skills needed, and the experience required. The JD is the most important document, because everything else is based on it.

### Step 2: Applying
You find the job on LinkedIn, Naukri, Indeed, a company website, or through a friend or referral, and send your resume.

### Step 3: ATS Screening
Most companies use an **ATS (Applicant Tracking System)**. It is software that reads your resume and checks it against the job description. It looks for:
- Matching keywords and skills
- Job titles, education, and experience
- A layout it can read properly

If your resume does not match or cannot be read, it may be rejected here, even if you are a good candidate.

### Step 4: Recruiter Resume Review
If you pass the software, a **recruiter or HR person** looks at your resume. They usually spend only **6 to 10 seconds** on the first look. They check your skills, projects, and whether you fit the role.

### Step 5: HR Phone Call or Screening
A recruiter calls or messages you. They ask simple questions:
- Tell me about yourself
- Why do you want this job?
- When can you join?
- What salary do you expect?

### Step 6: Online Test or Assignment
Many companies give a test, such as coding questions, aptitude, or a small project to do at home. This checks your real skills.

### Step 7: Technical Interview
A person from the team asks questions about your skills, projects, and problem solving. They may ask you to explain your own projects, so know them well.

### Step 8: Managerial or Final Round
The hiring manager checks if you will fit the team, how you think, and how you communicate.

### Step 9: HR Round
HR talks about salary, joining date, company rules, and any doubts you have.

### Step 10: Background Check and Offer
The company may verify your documents and details. If all is fine, you get an **offer letter** with the role, salary, and joining date.

### Step 11: Joining
You accept the offer, complete the paperwork, and start the job. 🎉

## 💡 Tips to Get More Interview Calls

**About your resume**
- Keep it to **one page** if you are a fresher
- Use simple headings like Education, Skills, Projects, and Experience
- Write projects with results, for example "Built a job recommendation app that ranks jobs by skill match"
- Use the same keywords as the job description, but only for skills you really have
- Always save and send the resume as a **PDF**

**About your portfolio**
- Show your **best 3 to 4 projects**, not everything
- Add a short description, the tools you used, and links to the code and live demo
- Put the portfolio link at the top of your resume

**About applying**
- Apply early, because many jobs get hundreds of applications in the first days
- Change your resume slightly for each job, using the Job Matcher
- Apply for jobs where your match score is good, and work on skills for the others
- Ask friends or seniors for **referrals**, because referred candidates often get noticed faster

**About your job search**
- Track every application, so you can follow up after about a week
- Send a short, polite follow-up message if you hear nothing
- Learn from rejections and improve your resume and skills

**About interviews**
- Know every project on your resume, because you will be asked about it
- Practice "Tell me about yourself" in 1 minute
- Keep a short list of questions to ask the interviewer

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

**Is this app free?**
Yes, it is free to use.

**Do I need coding knowledge?**
No. Open the live link, fill in the form, and download your files.

**What does ATS-friendly mean?**
ATS stands for Applicant Tracking System. It is software that companies use to scan resumes. An ATS-friendly resume has a simple layout so the software can read it correctly.

**What is a good match score?**
A higher score means your resume has more of the keywords the job asks for. Use it as a guide, not as a final judgment. Always be honest and add only skills you really have.

**Can I use the portfolio on my own website?**
Yes. It is a normal HTML file. You can host it on GitHub Pages, Netlify, or any hosting service.

**Can I change the resume design?**
Yes. Edit `src/resume_generator.py` to change the layout and sections.

**Why did my saved applications disappear after the app restarted?**
Free cloud hosting can reset local files. Download or note down important data, and consider a cloud database in the future.

**How many jobs should I apply for?**
Quality matters more than quantity. Apply to jobs that fit your skills, and customize your resume for each one.
